# Employee Security Risk Assessment

## Objective

Evaluate human-centered cybersecurity risks and identify appropriate security controls to reduce organizational exposure to social engineering attacks.

## Assessment Environment

- Platform: TryHackMe
- Room: Humans as Attack Vectors
- Module: Employees at Risk
- Role: Junior SOC Analyst
- Lab Status: Completed

## Risk Assessment Methodology

1. Identify potential employee security threats.
2. Evaluate potential business impact.
3. Determine appropriate preventive controls.
4. Recommend risk mitigation strategies.
5. Promote employee security awareness.

## Risk Assessment Matrix

The following matrix represents illustrative risks and recommended controls, rather than verified individual employee findings.

| Threat | Potential Impact | Suggested Risk Level | Recommended Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phishing | Credential theft | High | Email filtering and MFA |
| Social Engineering | Unauthorized access | High | Identity verification |
| Weak Passwords | Account compromise | High | Strong password policies |
| Password Reuse | Multiple account compromises | High | Password managers |
| Oversharing | Targeted attacks | Medium | Security awareness training |
| Suspicious Phone Calls | Information disclosure | Medium | Caller verification |

## SOC Analyst Response Procedures

- Investigate reported suspicious activity.
- Collect and preserve relevant evidence.
- Identify potentially affected accounts.
- Escalate high-risk incidents.
- Coordinate with IT and security teams.
- Document findings and recommendations.

## Evidence Limitations

The TryHackMe room was successfully completed. Individual employee assessment results were unavailable for retrieval after completion. The risk matrix provides illustrative security recommendations rather than confirmed lab findings.

## Conclusion

Human behavior represents a significant cybersecurity risk. Security awareness, strong authentication, effective reporting procedures, and proactive monitoring can help organizations reduce exposure to human-centered attacks.
